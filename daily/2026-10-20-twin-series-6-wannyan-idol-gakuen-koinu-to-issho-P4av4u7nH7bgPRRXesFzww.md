# Twin Series 6 - Wannyan Idol Gakuen + Koinu to Issho (GBA, 2004)

Caravan · Simulation · 4.7/5

Wannyan Idol Gakuen + Koinu to Issho Special is a Simulation game on Game Boy Advance (compilation). Wannyan Idol Gakuen lets players act as a young manager guiding cat girl idols to stardom. Players schedule vocal training, dance practice, public appearances, and balance idol lives while engaging in rhythm game segments for performances. Koinu to Issho Special is a special mode focused on daily pet care and interactions with a dog character. Gameplay features menu-driven scheduling and careful planning to avoid idol exhaustion. Story is whimsical with no deep narrative. Characters are the manager and cat girl idols plus dog elements.
