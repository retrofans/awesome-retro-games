# Super Gals! Kotobuki Ran (GBC, 2001)

Konami · Adventure · 4.7/5

Super Gals! Kotobuki Ran is an adventure game set in Shibuya where players control Ran Kotobuki, the self-proclaimed world's greatest gal. Gameplay involves managing her social status by completing missions: gathering clothes, hanging out with friends, and dating to outshine rivals in a competition for the No.1 Gal title. Story follows Ran doubting her status after a contest announcement, leading her to pursue more goods, dates, and fun while occasionally showing her sense of justice. Main characters include Ran Kotobuki, her best friends Miyu Yamazaki and Aya Hoshino, and rival Mami Honda.
