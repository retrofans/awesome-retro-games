# Stranger of Sword City (PSV, 2016)

Experience · rpg · 4.8/5

After surviving a plane crash, you awaken in Escario, a dark fantasy world of magic and technology. Build a party, explore treacherous labyrinths, and defeat powerful Lineage Type monsters to obtain Blood Crystals. Your choices of allies, including the Kingdom, Medell Co., and the Strangers Guild, influence your search for a way home.
