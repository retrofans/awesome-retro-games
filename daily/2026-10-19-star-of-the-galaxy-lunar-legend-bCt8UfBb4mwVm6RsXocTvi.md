# Star of the Galaxy - Lunar Legend (GBA, 2002)

Japan Art Media · RPG · 4.7/5

Lunar Legend is a remake of the classic Sega CD RPG Lunar: The Silver Star for Game Boy Advance. Players follow Alex, a young adventurer who dreams of becoming a hero like Dragonmaster Dyne, as he embarks on a quest to save the goddess Althena and protect the world of Lunar. The game features traditional turn-based combat, exploration of towns and dungeons, and a story focused on friendship and courage. This version streamlines some mechanics and adds new anime-style cutscenes while preserving the core narrative and characters.
