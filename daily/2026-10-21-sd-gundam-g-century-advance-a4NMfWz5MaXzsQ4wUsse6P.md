# SD Gundam G Century Advance (GBA, 2002)

Tom Create · Strategy · 4.7/5

SD Gundam G Generation Advance is a tactical strategy game featuring super-deformed mobile suits from the Gundam franchise. Players command squads of iconic mechs across turn-based battles, collecting and developing units through a generation system that lets pilots and machines grow stronger. The game covers multiple Gundam series including the Universal Century timeline, letting players recreate famous battles or forge new scenarios. Its deep customization and unit collection mechanics made it a standout entry in the G Generation series for portable play.
