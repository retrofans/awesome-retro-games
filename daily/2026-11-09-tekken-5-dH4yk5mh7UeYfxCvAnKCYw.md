# Tekken 5 (ARCADE, 2004)

Namco · Fighting · 4.6/5

Tekken 5 is a 3D fighting game developed and published by Namco featuring an extensive roster of over 40 characters from the Tekken universe. Gameplay follows the classic Tekken formula with 3D environments, fluid controls, sidestepping, and advanced combos, with new additions like Devil Within (a mini-game where players fight Devil clones) and Arcade History mode (emulating classic arcade Tekken games). Story mode includes character-specific narratives and endings. It stands out for its balanced gameplay, improved juggling, character customization, and status as a fan-favorite that revitalized the series with deep character stories and high production values.
