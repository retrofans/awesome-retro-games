# Puyo Puyo 4 - Magic Party (N64, 1999)

Compile · Puzzle · 4.8/5

Puyo Puyo 4 retains the core gameplay of Puyo Puyo 2 and adds super attacks: clearing chains fills a charge meter that lets players unleash them. It also introduces a mode with selectable field sizes; the Nintendo 64 version supports four players and a Transfer Pak mode that unlocks more artwork by connecting Pocket Puyo Puyo Sun.
