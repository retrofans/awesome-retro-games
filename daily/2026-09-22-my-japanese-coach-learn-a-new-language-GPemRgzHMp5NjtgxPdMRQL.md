# My Japanese Coach - Learn a New Language (DS, 2007)

Rocket Company · Educational · 4.9/5

My Japanese Coach is a language-learning game designed to teach Japanese to English speakers on the Nintendo DS. The game uses flashcards, mini-games, and interactive lessons to help players learn hiragana, katakana, kanji, and basic vocabulary and grammar. Progress is tracked through a structured curriculum that adapts to the player's skill level. The touch screen and stylus controls make learning feel natural and engaging for portable study sessions.
