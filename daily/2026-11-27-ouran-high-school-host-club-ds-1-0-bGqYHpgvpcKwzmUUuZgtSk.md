# Ouran High School Host Club DS 1.0 (DS, 2007)

Idea Factory · Simulation/Adventure · 4.6/5

Based on the popular Ouran High School Host Club anime and manga, this Nintendo DS game lets players experience life at the prestigious academy. You create a female protagonist who encounters the famous Host Club members including Tamaki, Kyoya, and the Hitachiin twins. The game features mini-games, relationship-building mechanics, and multiple story routes.
