# Dragon Warrior IV (NES, 1990)

Chunsoft · RPG · 4.8/5

Role-playing game for NES/Famicom. Divided into five chapters with unique heroes: Ragnar (knight seeking missing kids), Alena (princess proving strength), Torneko (merchant), and others. The hero joins the party and travels to stop Estark's resurrection of evil. Combat uses the AI party system where companions act independently. Standout: Pioneering character-driven storytelling, large world, and memorable companions like Healie (Healslime). Characters: Hero (male/female), Ragnar, Alena, Torneko, Kiryl, Borya, etc.
