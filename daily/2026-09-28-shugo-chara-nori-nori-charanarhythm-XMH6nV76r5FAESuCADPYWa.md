# Shugo Chara! - Nori Nori! CharanaRhythm (DS, 2009)

Konami · Rhythm · 4.8/5

Shugo Chara! - Nori Nori! CharanaRhythm is a rhythm game on Nintendo DS featuring dance-like tapping mechanics on the touch screen to match symbols and score points. Story mode follows Yua Sakurai's CD release, involving her Guardian Character Cecil, transformations like ?-Transformation and Wonderful Singer, and appearances from characters like Nagihiko (now the Jack), Kairi, and mini-games with Ran, Miki, and Suu. Playing these mini-games earns coins for unlocking inventory items such as screenshots and draft drawings. The game stands out with its fun, energetic music-based dance rhythm gameplay and loyal Shugo Chara series character integration.
