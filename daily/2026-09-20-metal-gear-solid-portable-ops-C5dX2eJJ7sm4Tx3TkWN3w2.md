# Metal Gear Solid: Portable Ops (PSP, 2006)

Kojima Productions · Action · 4.7/5

Metal Gear Solid: Portable Ops is a tactical stealth action game that follows Naked Snake (Big Boss) in 1970 Colombia after the events of Metal Gear Solid 3. Players recruit enemy soldiers to build their own army through a unique squad-based system, managing units across missions. The game features both single-player campaign and competitive multiplayer modes. It bridges the story between Snake Eater and the original Metal Gear, exploring Big Boss's transformation and the origins of FOXHOUND.
