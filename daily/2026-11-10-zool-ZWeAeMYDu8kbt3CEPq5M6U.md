# Zool (SNES, 1994)

Gremlin Graphics · Platformer · 4.9/5

Zool is a fast-paced 2D platformer featuring Zool, the gremlin ninja from the Nth Dimension. Stranded on Earth, he tackles seven diverse worlds to prove his skills through precise platforming, combat, and item collection. Mechanics include running, jumping, shooting, and special moves, with embedded mini-games adding variety. The game features colorful, themed levels and challenging bosses. It stands out on SNES for its speed, vibrant visuals, and attempt to capture the mascot platformer craze, offering unique content in console ports.
