# John Madden Football - Championship Edition (MD, 1992)

Electronic Arts · Sports · 4.6/5

John Madden Football - Championship Edition is a classic American football simulation for Sega Mega Drive (Genesis). Gameplay involves realistic 3D top-down or split-screen field views with detailed play calling, player controls for passing (spin, dive, speed burst), tackling, and audibles. The championship-caliber teams from 1966-1991 (38 historic squads plus two All-Madden teams) feature unique strengths, weaknesses, and ratings based on real players (identified by number). Key modes include preseason, regular season, playoffs, and sudden death overtime.
