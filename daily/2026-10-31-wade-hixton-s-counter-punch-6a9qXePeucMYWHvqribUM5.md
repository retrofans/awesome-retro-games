# 维德-汉克斯的反击 Wade Hixton's Counter Punch (GBA, 2004)

Inferno Games · Fighting · 4.7/5

Wade Hixton's Counter Punch is a boxing game on the Game Boy Advance where players control the everyman Wade Hixton in third-person fights against wacky opponents. Gameplay involves dodging and counter-punching in close combat, with upgradable movesets from allies. Story: Stranded in a small town after his car breaks down, Wade meets quirky locals and rivals for a boxing tournament. Characters include Wade, the stereotypical pimp Sweetness (who speaks in jive slang), the witch doctor Jobu, and a raver girl. It stands out as a creative, funny take on Punch-Out!! with hand-drawn cartoon graphics, addictive combat, and bizarre humor on the GBA.
