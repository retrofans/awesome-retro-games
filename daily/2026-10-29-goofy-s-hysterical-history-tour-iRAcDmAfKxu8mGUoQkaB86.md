# Goofy's Hysterical History Tour (MD, 1993)

Absolute Entertainment · Platformer · 4.8/5

Goofy's Hysterical History Tour is a 2D platformer for Sega Mega Drive where players control Goofy as a janitor at Ludwig von Drake's History Museum. Goofy uses the Extend-O-Glove to clean exhibits, hit enemies, grab blocks for swinging over pits, and collect yellow balloons for points or blue balls for damage. He navigates through four time-travel eras: Prehistoric Times, Wild West, Colonial America, and Medieval Times, recovering sabotaged exhibits and defeating the villain Pete. The game features side-scrolling platforming mechanics with humorous puns and loading screen jokes.
