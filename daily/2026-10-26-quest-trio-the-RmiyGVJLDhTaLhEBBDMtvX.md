# Quest Trio, The (DS, 2008)

Mistic Software iWin Game Brains · Puzzle · 4.9/5

The Quest Trio is a Nintendo DS puzzle compilation featuring three connected games. Jewel Quest Expeditions is a match-3 puzzle where players swap adjacent colored jewels to form lines of three or more; cleared jewels drop down and gold the board squares, requiring all squares to turn gold within time limits or lose a life. It includes adventure-themed levels and story elements with protagonist Rupert Pack seeking a legendary jewel board in Africa. Mah Jong Quest Expeditions is a mahjong solitaire adventure puzzle where players remove matching tiles in pairs to clear the board, with story progression through global quests and humorous narrative.
