# Thor - God of Thunder (DS, 2011)

WayForward Technologies · Action · 4.8/5

Thor: God of Thunder is a 2D side-scrolling action-platformer on Nintendo DS where players control Thor wielding his hammer Mjölnir to battle massive Norse mythological enemies across realms like Vanaheim, Jötunheim, and Helheim. Gameplay features simple combat with button-assigned basic attacks, elemental storm powers (lightning, thunder, wind), and God Power attacks activated via the touch screen after charging an energy gauge; chapters are structured in 3 acts each with 7 worlds total and boss fights in the final act of each.
