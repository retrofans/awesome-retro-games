# River City: Rival Showdown (SNES, 1994)

Almanic · Action · 4.7/5

Shin Nekketsu Kouha: Kunio-tachi no Banka is a side-scrolling beat 'em up in the Kunio-kun series. Players control Kunio, Riki, and later their girlfriends Misako and Kyoko as they fight through stages to clear their names after being framed for a hit-and-run. The game features character switching mechanics when health is low, special attacks, and a more serious storyline compared to previous entries. It stands out for its emotional narrative involving themes of innocence, betrayal, and justice in the high school delinquent world.
