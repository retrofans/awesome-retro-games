# Ochaken no Heya DS 4 (DS, 2009)

HuneX · Simulation · 4.8/5

Ochaken no Heya DS 4 is a virtual life simulation game in the Ocha-Ken franchise, where players manage adorable tea-leaf-eared dogs and cats in a customizable house and garden. Gameplay centers on minigames for obtaining furniture and items, house decoration, pet interactions, and casual activities like watching TV or playing games. The story involves cute vignettes and light-hearted pet adventures in Ocha-ken Land, emphasizing customization, adorable character interactions, and relaxing virtual pet management.
