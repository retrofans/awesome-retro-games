# Bugs Bunny in - Crazy Castle 4 (GBC, 2000)

Kemco · Puzzle/Platformer · 4.9/5

Bugs Bunny in Crazy Castle 4 is a puzzle-platformer where players guide Bugs Bunny through multi-floor castle levels collecting carrots and keys. The gameplay focuses on avoiding enemies rather than defeating them, requiring careful timing and route planning. Each stage presents a maze-like environment where Bugs must reach the exit while dodging classic Looney Tunes characters. The game features over 60 levels with increasing difficulty and puzzle complexity.
