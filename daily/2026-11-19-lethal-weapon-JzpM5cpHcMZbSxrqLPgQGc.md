# Lethal Weapon (SNES, 1992)

Ocean Software · Action · 4.7/5

Lethal Weapon is an action game for the SNES/Super Famicom based on the film series. It is a side-scrolling platformer where players control either Martin Riggs or Roger Murtaugh, the partners from the movies. Riggs has a higher fire rate but lower jump height, while Murtaugh has a higher jump but slower firing. The player must complete four missions in any order before the final one: stopping a smuggling ring at the harbor, defusing bombs in a shopping mall and subway station, rescuing Leo Getz, and pursuing the villain Jack Travis in the finale. Gameplay involves running, shooting enemies with handguns and grenades, and karate kicks in city and level stages.
