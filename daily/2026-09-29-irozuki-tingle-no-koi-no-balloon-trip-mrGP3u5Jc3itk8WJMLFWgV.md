# Irozuki Tingle no Koi no Balloon Trip (DS, 2009)

Vanpool · Adventure · 4.9/5

Ripened Tingle's Balloon Trip of Love is an adventure game where a lonely 35-year-old bachelor named Tingle, inspired by The Wizard of Oz, enters a magical picture book world after ordering a mysterious book. He transforms into Tingle and teams up with Kakashi the scarecrow, Buriki the tin robot woman, and Lion the lion to journey across the land, solving puzzles using their unique abilities and your stylus controls to tap and interact with the world.
