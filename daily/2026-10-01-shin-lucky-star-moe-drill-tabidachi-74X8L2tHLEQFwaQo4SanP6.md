# Shin Lucky Star Moe Drill - Tabidachi (DS, 2008)

Vridge · Educational · 4.9/5

An educational game based on the Lucky Star anime series featuring characters like Konata, Kagami, Tsukasa, and Miyuki. Players engage in quiz-style learning exercises covering Japanese language, math, and general knowledge subjects presented through visual novel-style interactions. The game uses the DS touch screen for input and features voice acting from the original anime cast. It combines educational drills with the charm of the Lucky Star universe to make studying more engaging.
