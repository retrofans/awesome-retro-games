# America Daitouryou Senkyo (NES, 1988)

Hect · Strategy · 4.8/5

America Daitouryou Senkyo is a text-heavy simulation in which the player selects a candidate and runs for the presidency of the United States. The player takes liberal, conservative, or centrist positions on four issues, manages campaign funds, and uses opinion polls, speeches, and state primaries to advance the campaign. Starting at a party convention in February 1988, the goal is to reach the final election results in November without going bankrupt.
