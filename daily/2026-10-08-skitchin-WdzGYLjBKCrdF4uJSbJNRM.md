# Skitchin' (MD, 1994)

EA Canada · Racing · 4.6/5

Skitchin' is a 1994 racing game for Sega Genesis where the player controls an inline skater racing through 12 cities in a world skitchin' championship. Gameplay involves skating, skitching onto moving vehicles for speed boosts, performing tricks on ramps, and engaging in unarmed or armed combat with opponents using pickups like nunchaku, chains, pipes, crowbars, whips, and baseball bats. Story follows the skater advancing by finishing in the top five while defending and attacking rivals in a dangerous tournament spanning Vancouver to San Francisco and beyond.
