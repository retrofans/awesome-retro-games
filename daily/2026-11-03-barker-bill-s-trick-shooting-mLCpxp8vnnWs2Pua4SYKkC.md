# Barker Bill's Trick Shooting (NES, 1990)

Nintendo R&D1 · Shooter · 4.9/5

Barker Bill's Trick Shooting is a light gun shooter for the NES/Famicom featuring four carnival-style game modes: Balloon Saloon (shoot balloons while avoiding the Duck Hunt dog), Flying Saucers (shoot plates tossed by Barker Bill and assistant Trixie), Window Pains (shoot falling objects through window panes), and Fun Follies. The player must use the NES Zapper to hit targets within time limits for high scores. Barker Bill is the mustachioed circus ringmaster narrator, with Trixie as his bunny girl assistant who waves goodbye at the end and applauds results.
