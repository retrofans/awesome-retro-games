# Famista 3 (GB, 1993)

Namco · Sports · 4.7/5

Famista 3 is a baseball simulation game where players manage teams and compete in matches. Gameplay includes batting, pitching, and fielding with simple controls suitable for handheld play. Story mode allows selecting from 14 teams including edit teams. Characters feature real Japanese baseball stars like Ichiro (as Suzuki) and Namco Stars all-star team. Modes include league battles, all-star games, team editing, and scorebook. It stands out with battery backup for team creation and original player creation via barcode reader.
