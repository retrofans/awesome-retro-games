# Puzzle Series Vol. 6 - Illust Logic (DS, 2006)

Hudson Soft · Puzzle · 4.8/5

Puzzle Series Vol. 6 - Illust Logic is a logic puzzle game from Hudson Soft featuring Illust Logic (a specialized nonogram variant) alongside Colorful Logic. Gameplay involves solving grid-based logic puzzles by shading cells according to numbered clues on the edges, with the unique Illust Logic twist of revealing pixel-art illustrations upon completion. There are no story modes or characters; it stands out with its colorful artistic puzzles, DS-specific touch screens and stylus support for precise input, and the ability to enjoy both classic black-and-white nonograms and vibrant color variants in one package.
