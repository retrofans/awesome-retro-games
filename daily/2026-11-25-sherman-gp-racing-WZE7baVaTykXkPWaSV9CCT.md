# Sherman GP Racing (N64, 1997)

Rare · Racing · 4.8/5

Diddy Kong Racing is a kart racing game for the Nintendo 64 developed by Rare. Players control one of ten animal characters (including Diddy Kong, Banjo the Bear, and Conker the Squirrel) through five worlds filled with car, hovercraft, or airplane tracks. Adventure Mode follows the story where the group races to defeat the wizard pig Wizpig and his henchmen. Battle modes allow up to four players in deathmatch, capture-the-flag, and egg-capture arenas. It stands out for its three vehicle types, zippers for boosts, colored power-up balloons with upgrades, and Banjo and Conker's early appearances before their flagship titles.
