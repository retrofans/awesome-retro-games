# Assault City (SMS, 1990)

Sanritsu · Rail shooter · 4.9/5

Assault City is a rail shooter for the Sega Master System where the player controls Joe, a survivor of a cybernetic revolt. The story follows an AI uprising that turns robots against humanity, forcing humans into a resistance to destroy the control system and protect the last of mankind. Gameplay involves fast-paced scrolling action as the camera moves linearly rightward, with the player shooting enemies from a moving vehicle or position while managing a life bar. Characters are primarily Joe as the protagonist and various hostile robots and war machines.
