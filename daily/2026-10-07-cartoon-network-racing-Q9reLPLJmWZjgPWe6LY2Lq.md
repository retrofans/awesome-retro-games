# Cartoon Network Racing (DS, 2006)

Firebrand Games · Racing · 4.8/5

Cartoon Network Racing is a kart racing game for the Nintendo DS featuring characters from Cartoon Network series. Players select a driver and co-driver to race in tournaments or battle arenas, collecting toon coins and unlocking courses from shows like Dexter's Laboratory, The Powerpuff Girls, Johnny Bravo, Courage the Cowardly Dog, Cow and Chicken, and I Am Weasel. Gameplay involves classic Mario Kart-style racing with power-ups, weapons, and toon abilities; there are 16-20 characters including Dexter, Blossom, Courage, Cow, and Johnny Bravo, each with unique karts and stats.
