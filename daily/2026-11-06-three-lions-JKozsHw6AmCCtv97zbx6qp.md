# Three Lions (GBC, 1999)

Z-Axis Tarantula Studios · Sports · 4.7/5

Three Lions is an official Football (soccer) Simulation/Sports game for Game Boy Color. Gameplay involves controlling players in single-player tournament or multiplayer exhibition matches, with accurate English FA kits, real 1998 World Cup roster (e.g., Beckham, Sheringham, Gascoigne), and detailed mechanics like passing, shooting, tackling, and dribbling on 8 pitch variants. Main characters include the England national team squad. Standout features: 12 AI opponents with unique styles, customizable tournament brackets, and licensed authenticity that makes it feel like controlling the real Three Lions team to win the World Cup.
