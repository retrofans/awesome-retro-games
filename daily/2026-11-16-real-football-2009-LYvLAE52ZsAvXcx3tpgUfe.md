# Real Football 2009 (DS, 2008)

Gameloft · Sports · 4.9/5

Real Football 2009 is a handheld soccer simulation game. Gameplay features standard match controls with DS-specific touch screen support for passes (lines or curves), lobs, shots with power charging, and throw-ins via device tilt. Game modes include exhibition matches, cups, leagues, penalty shootouts, training, and multiplayer up to 4 players. Story mode focuses on league progression and tournament challenges. Characters include over 300 licensed real teams with real player names and stadiums. What makes it stand out is its revolutionary touch screen gesture controls that are intuitive yet novel for handheld soccer play, along with a large licensed team roster and smooth 3D graphics.
