# Instant Feeling Dragon Ball (DS, 2006)

Mitchell Corporation · Puzzle · 4.8/5

Magnetica (Shunkan Puzz Loop / 瞬感龙珠) is a twisty-path puzzle game where a chain of magnetic marbles rolls toward a black hole. Players flick marbles using the DS stylus to create 3+ matching chains that explode and clear the path. Same-color marbles attract and pull chains to prevent gaps. Challenge mode features endless rolling chains; Quest requires specific goals on varied tracks; Puzzle allows static setups with limited marbles. Single- and multiplayer modes available. It stands out for its intuitive touch controls and addictive chain-reaction physics.
