# Puzzle Series - Jigsaw Puzzle - Koneko Mekuri Hen (DS, 2006)

Hudson Soft · Puzzle · 4.8/5

Jigsaw Puzzle - Koneko Mekuri Hen is a casual puzzle game for Nintendo DS in the Puzzle Series. Players assemble jigsaw puzzles of cute kitten photographs from Comin baby animal calendars, with three difficulty levels based on the number of pieces (20 to 56). Solved puzzles display on the top screen and can be used as calendar decorations. There are normal puzzles and weekly puzzles that unlock progressively with new ones appearing on certain days. It stands out for its simple, relaxing gameplay focused on cute kawaii cat themes, with no complex story or characters involved.
