# Bomberman Max 2 - Blue Advance (GBA, 2002)

Hudson Soft · Action · 4.9/5

Bomberman Max 2 - Blue Advance is an action game on Game Boy Advance in which players control the classic Bomberman character as he is shrunken to miniature size by the villain Mujoe using his Mini-Mini Device. Players must navigate through themed mini-worlds such as gardens, libraries, and laboratories, destroying enemies and obstacles with bombs while collecting items to upgrade their abilities and return to normal size. Key mechanics include using Charabombs to befriend and deploy special helpers that grant abilities like walking through blocks or super jumps, along with puzzle-solving elements to reach the shrink ray reversal.
