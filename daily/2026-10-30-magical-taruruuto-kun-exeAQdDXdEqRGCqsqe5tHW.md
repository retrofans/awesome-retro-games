# Magical Taruruuto-kun (GB, 1991)

Radish Games · Platformer · 4.6/5

Magical Taruruuto-kun is a platformer based on the manga character Taruruuto. Players control the young magician as he progresses through side-scrolling levels using magical attacks and power-ups. The game features colorful pixel art typical of early Game Boy titles. Taruruuto must defeat enemies and bosses while collecting items to advance. The gameplay emphasizes action over complexity, making it accessible to younger players.
