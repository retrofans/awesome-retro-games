Sega · Action/Adventure/Platformer · 4.9/5

The Jungle Book on Game Gear is a platformer-adventure game based on Disney's animated classic, where players control Mowgli navigating through the jungle. The game features side-scrolling action with platforming challenges, enemy encounters with characters like Shere Khan and King Louie, and collectible items to help progression. The game captures the charm of the Disney film while providing challenging gameplay suited to the handheld platform.
