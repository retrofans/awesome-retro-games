# Super Fire Pro Wrestling (SNES, 1994)

Human Entertainment · Fighting · 4.9/5

Super Fire Pro Wrestling Special is a wrestling simulation game where you play as Morio Smith, an aspiring pro wrestler navigating the violent, emotional highs and lows of the industry in a 10-chapter story mode written by Goichi Suda. In Champion Road, you rise through promotions, face rivals like Sting, Rick Rude, and The Undertaker, and unlock hidden characters and Gruesome Fighting Mode. Gameplay features an innovative timing-based grapple system with three levels of moves (weak, medium, strong), strikes on downed opponents, aerial attacks, and ring-out options, plus modes like One Night Match (singles/tag), Battle Royale, Tournament, Open League, and create-a-wrestler.
