# Battle Dodge Ball II (SNES, 1993)

Nova Games · Sports · 4.8/5

Battle Dodge Ball II is a sports arcade-style dodgeball game for the Super Famicom. Players select teams composed of heroes or villains from Ultraman, Kamen Rider, Gundam, and Mazinger Z series, each with unique special moves for damaging opponents. Gameplay involves throwing a dodgeball back and forth on the court to reduce enemy HP until one team is eliminated. Story mode lets you select courts and face home teams, with progression by stealing captains after wins. Characters include Ultraman Great, Ultraseven, Kamen Rider Black RX, Mazinger Z, and various Gundam models.
